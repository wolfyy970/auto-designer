The design should surface, at the moment the patient is choosing a clinician, the information directories currently hide: whether the practice is accepting new patients, when the next appointment actually is, what languages the clinicians speak, what physical and communication accommodations the office offers, and what scope of practice the clinician handles. The failure mode to avoid: presenting a curated-looking list whose underlying confidence is no greater than the directory's, where the patient still cannot tell whether what's shown is accurate.

The design should treat the patient as choosing a long-term care relationship, not transacting on a single appointment. The design move should reflect that establishing care is a multi-visit decision — the first visit is the start of the relationship, not the success criterion. The failure mode to avoid: a flow optimized for fastest-path-to-any-booking that produces a poor match, a discontinued relationship by visit two, and a patient back at the start of the search.

The design should let patients with richer match criteria — chronic conditions requiring ongoing management, mental-health needs, women's-health needs, geriatric needs, family-medicine needs across multiple people — express what they're searching for at a grain finer than "primary care." The failure mode to avoid: a search experience tuned to the median request that disadvantages the patients whose needs are most specific and whose stakes for a good match are highest.

The design should make honest the gap between what the design knows about a practice and what is unverified. When acceptance status, appointment availability, or language coverage is stale, the design should show that it's stale rather than presenting it with the same confidence as verified data. The failure mode to avoid: showing every field with the same surface confidence and breaking trust the first time the patient calls a practice and learns the data was wrong.

The design should let the patient see the alternatives their landscape actually contains alongside the in-network directory, without prescribing which one is right. The failure mode to avoid: framing one alternative as the answer, narrowing what the patient considers, or hiding alternatives the patient would otherwise reach.
