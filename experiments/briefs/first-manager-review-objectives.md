The design must produce a review that improves the employee's work and the working relationship. The failure mode to avoid: an experience that produces well-written reviews the employee experiences as performative or unfair.

The design must reduce the manager's anxiety enough that they actually write a real review, not a hedged one. The failure mode to avoid: anxiety-reduction that produces a confident-but-blander review than the situation deserves.

The design must surface the manager's own observations and notes back to them, in ways that make the connection between specific moments and aggregate feedback. The failure mode to avoid: AI-generated language that the manager hasn't actually verified.

The design must respect that the review will be calibrated against peer-managers' ratings. The failure mode to avoid: a design that produces reviews that look good in isolation but distort calibration.

The design must support the conversation that follows the writing, not just the writing itself. The failure mode to avoid: optimizing the review document at the expense of the conversation the document is meant to enable.
