The intake surface should externalize the form's current state at every step — what has been asked, what has been answered, what remains, and what document is still missing — so the applicant is never carrying the application in their head across screens or sessions. The failure mode to avoid: an externalization so heavy that the screen reads as a status dashboard rather than an application, leaving the applicant unsure which surface is the one they act on.

The post-submission experience should communicate state legibly — confirmation that the submission was received, a named next step the applicant can act on, and a clearly indicated horizon for the determination. The failure mode to avoid: false reassurance that the application is on track when state-side processing has stalled or is waiting on something not yet surfaced; the design should be honest about delay rather than performing progress.

The interface should carry trust signals that are legible to populations with valid reasons to distrust government processes — mixed-status immigrant families, people previously involved with the criminal-legal system, people previously denied. That includes plain disclosure of what is collected, what is shared with the state, and what is not shared elsewhere. The failure mode to avoid: trust theater that reads reassuring to the agency's voice but reads as coded surveillance or data-harvesting to the audience it is meant to reassure.

The experience should fit the device, network, and document reality the applicant actually has — phone-shaped, sometimes shared, on intermittent data, with documentation the applicant may need to gather over several sessions. The failure mode to avoid: a resilience pattern that quietly tolerates so many interruptions that the applicant loses track of whether they have actually submitted anything.

The submission output should be one the state eligibility worker can act on — complete enough that adjudication doesn't bounce back to the applicant for more information, structured enough that the worker can read it under the time pressure their queue imposes. The failure mode to avoid: an output optimized for applicant ease that pushes rework downstream and slows determinations for every applicant in the queue.
