The design must reduce evictions of tenants who have a legal defense or a basis for negotiation. The failure mode to avoid: helping tenants who don't have a defense feel optimistic in ways that delay them from negotiating or moving when those options are best.

The design must surface the defenses and procedural arguments that apply to the tenant's specific facts. The failure mode to avoid: dumping the universe of possible defenses on someone who needs to know which two apply to them.

The design must produce filed paperwork (answer, discovery, motions) on time and in the format the court accepts. The failure mode to avoid: producing legally-sound text that won't be filed because the tenant didn't know how to file or missed the deadline.

The design must respect the tenant's distress and limited bandwidth. The failure mode to avoid: long-form intake that drives the tenant away before they have a usable plan.

The design must coordinate with legal-aid clinics where they exist and the tenant qualifies, rather than substituting for them. The failure mode to avoid: serving the tenants who don't qualify for aid while creating a parallel system that drains demand from over-loaded clinics.
