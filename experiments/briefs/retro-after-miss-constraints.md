Employee data and retro content are sensitive. The architecture must respect that retro content may include statements employees would not want exposed outside the team — to leadership, to HR, to future employers. Data-retention and access policies are required.

Tool integrations vary: Jira, Linear, GitHub, Slack, Teams, Miro, Confluence, Notion. The design must integrate broadly or operate above the layer.

Employee-surveillance ethics apply. The design must not become an instrument of managerial pattern-detection that the team did not consent to.

GDPR/CCPA apply where the team includes employees in those jurisdictions. Cross-border data residency may matter for some companies.

The design must respect the team's existing culture and rhythm. Imposing a new ceremony on top of an established retro practice is high-friction; the design should be able to operate inside the format the team already uses.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) is a floor. Some team members have disabilities; the retro experience must work for them.

Psychological-safety claims must be defensible. The design cannot promise outcomes (team psychological safety) it cannot deliver from the tool alone; over-promising is a documented failure pattern in this space.
